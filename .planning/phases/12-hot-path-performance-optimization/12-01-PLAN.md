<objective>
Eliminate per-request heap allocations in ONNX tensor construction and remove dead audio buffer copies in hephaestus-core pipeline.rs.

Purpose: The inference hot path currently clones Vec data into Array2 tensors on every request. Switching to ArrayView2 borrows the existing data without copying. Removing the dead raw_samples field from PreparedAudio eliminates a ~1.9MB clone per ASR window. These are the highest-impact memory optimizations in the codebase, directly addressing rules/ violations (anti-clone-excessive, own-borrow-over-clone, mem-zero-copy).

Output: Updated pipeline.rs with zero-copy tensor construction in run_onnx_inference, no pre-clone in EmbeddingsPipeline::execute, no raw_samples in PreparedAudio, direct Vec consumption in CTC prepare, and ArrayView in Whisper decode loop.
</objective>
