## Phase Goal

**As a** Hephaestus operator, **I want to** have models without ONNX exports automatically converted and cached by a persistent Forge service, **so that** I can deploy any HuggingFace model and the full resolution chain handles conversion transparently.

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-R01 | Tampering | forge.rs HttpForgeClient response deserialization | medium | mitigate | Strongly-typed ForgeResponse with Deserialize — rejects unexpected JSON shapes; errors mapped to ForgeConversion with detail |
| T-05-R02 | DoS | forge.rs reqwest timeout | medium | mitigate | reqwest::Client configured with FORGE_TIMEOUT_SECS (default 600s, per D-04); prevents unbounded blocking on Forge |
| T-05-R03 | Information Disclosure | error.rs ForgeConversion reason field | low | accept | Reason may include Forge response body text; acceptable for internal service — no external exposure. Operators need full error context for debugging. |
| T-05-R04 | Tampering | main.rs FORGE_URL env var | low | accept | Internal k8s env config; URL validated by reqwest during request. Malicious URL would fail with connection error, not cause data corruption. |
</threat_model>
